Why pure modes?

Compositional diffusion often captures individual concepts while failing to realize them jointly in the final sample. Rather than repeatedly correcting noisy intermediate predictions, TILT asks what final clean samples should receive high reward and derives the corresponding inference-time steering direction.

The key distinction: correcting an intermediate denoised estimate need not produce the desired distribution after diffusion dynamics. We formulate the objective directly at the final clean sample.

Model-Intrinsic Reward

The pretrained model already contains evidence about whether concepts occur jointly or are dominated by individual constituents. TILT turns this contrast into an intrinsic reward and uses it for inference-time steering.

Beyond Images

The same idea extends beyond text-to-image generation because the reward is obtained from the generative model itself rather than a modality-specific external scorer.
